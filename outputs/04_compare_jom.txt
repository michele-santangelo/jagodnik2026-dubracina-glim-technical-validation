A caveat on interpretation: the very-old-relict layer contains a few
polygons of several km2 which overlap almost everything, so a criterion
based on the largest overlapping area would be biased towards them.
Intersection over union is used instead, which selects the polygon of
comparable size and position.

3b - Error index E of Carrara et al. (1992), pairwise
-----------------------------------------------------
E = (A u B - A n B) / (A u B) for two corresponding polygons, 0 for a
perfect match and 1 for none. It is the complement of the intersection
over union, E = 1 - IoU, so the two carry the same information; what
differs is where it is applied.

Applied PAIRWISE, as Carrara et al. intend and as Santangelo et al.
(2015) apply it, E measures the discrepancy between a previously mapped
polygon and its counterpart here. Applied GLOBALLY to the two unions
over the domain it measures something else - how much of the combined
mapped area is not shared - and it is then dominated by whatever one
inventory maps and the other does not. The global value is reported in
section 2 and in script 12; the pairwise distribution is reported here.
A polygon with no counterpart has E = 1 by definition and is kept in,
since dropping it would flatter the result.

4 - Confirmation and increment over the shared domain
-----------------------------------------------------
The converse of section 1. For every landslide of the present inventory
that reaches the shared domain, the fraction of its own area - clipped
to that domain - which the previous inventory covers. A landslide
covered over at least half of its area was already mapped; one below
that threshold is an addition. The threshold is a convention, so the
count with no overlap at all is reported as well, as a lower bound that
does not depend on it.

Depth class does not separate the two groups; size and generation do.
The increment is not a deeper reading of the same landslides, it is a
different population: large, old, subdued features whose expression is
an arrangement of slope facets over hundreds of metres rather than the
sharp morphology of a recent failure. That is what the pseudo-
stereoscopic interpretation is for, and it is why no external dataset
can validate this part of the inventory: for this class of feature the
method used here is more sensitive than the alternatives available to
check it against.
