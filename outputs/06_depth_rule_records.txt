labelling slip or a consistent interpretive choice.

Reading: every type pair is deep-seated in 100 % of cases when the source
area was delineated separately - except xdf+df, which is shallow in 100 %
of cases and deep-seated in none. Note that the other flow-type pairs,
xsef+sef and xf+f, follow the rule, so the exception is specific to debris
flows and not to flows in general.

This is a defensible interpretation: the two polygons of a debris flow are
the concave detachment/transport zone and the fan deposit, which is not the
scarp of a rotational or translational failure and carries no implication
about the depth of a failure surface. The consistency of the convention
(80 out of 80) argues that the data are right and the rule as written in
the Technical Validation section is incomplete.

What to decide for each record
------------------------------
The violations are not all the same problem:

  2poly_shallow - the source area WAS delineated separately, so under the
      stated rule the landslide is deep-seated. Either the depth value is
      wrong, or these are cases where the interpreter deliberately mapped
      a scarp for a shallow failure, in which case the rule in the text
      does not describe what was actually done and must be reworded.

  1poly_deep - a deep-seated landslide whose source area was not
      delineated. Check whether the missing polygon was lost in editing
      or whether the deposit alone was mapped on purpose.

  mixed_depth - unambiguous data error: the two polygons of one landslide
      cannot have different depth classes.

from a deliberate mapping choice.
