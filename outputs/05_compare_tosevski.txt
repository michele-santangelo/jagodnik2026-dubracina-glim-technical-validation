Tosevski mapped only soil slides in the flysch weathering zone and states
that depth of the failure surface was left for future work, so his slides
carry no depth class. A difference in the depth attribute is therefore not
a disagreement between the two inventories.

The two inventories are complementary rather than competing: Tosevski
mapped a single process type over 18.55 km2 with field verification, and
almost every one of his slides is recovered by the new G-LIM, which maps
many more process types and several generations over the whole catchment.
