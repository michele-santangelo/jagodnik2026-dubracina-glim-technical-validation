note         grid areas computed from the projected coordinates are about 300 ppm larger, because HTRS96/TM has k0 = 0.9999 on the central meridian at 16.5 deg E and the valley lies about 145 km west of it

This paragraph belongs in the Data Records: without it a reader who
recomputes the areas from the projected coordinates gets numbers about
300 ppm larger and has no way to tell whether the published field is wrong.

Every figure below is given on both bases. Note that all ratios - density,
percentage of the study area - are identical on the two bases to the
precision reported, because the scale factor cancels in a ratio of two
areas measured the same way. Only absolute areas differ, in the fourth
significant figure.

The study area is the measured area of DRV_Boundary, 38.4 km2, which is the
domain that was actually mapped. The 43.6 km2 of the submitted manuscript is
the hydrological basin area (43.55 km2 in Tosevski 2013); the difference is
the sub-horizontal karst surfaces, deliberately excluded. Every density and
coverage figure below refers to the mapped domain.
