Why the index of agreement is low while 95.6 % of their polygons are
recovered. The two measure different things. Recovery asks what share of
THEIR polygons this inventory covers, and answers 95.6 % of the polygons
and 63.0 % of their area. The index of agreement is symmetric: it divides
the shared area by the union, and the union is dominated by the 8.20 km2
this inventory maps and the previous one does not. The index therefore
measures how much has been added, not how far the two disagree where both
looked. The ground neither maps, 5.59 km2, does not enter it at all.

A caution on the restricted comparison. Dropping to the old class alone
removes the rockfall layer from OUR side while leaving the rock-fall
chutes and cliff sectors on THEIRS, which is why the ground mapped only
by the previous inventory grows from 1.00 to 1.77 km2 - visibly, along
the north-eastern carbonate margin. The like-for-like restriction is the
old class WITH the rockfall layer, and on that subset the index of
agreement is the highest of the three, because removing the very old and
relict polygons removes area that is ours alone without removing
agreement. That is the same result the increment analysis reports from
the other direction.
