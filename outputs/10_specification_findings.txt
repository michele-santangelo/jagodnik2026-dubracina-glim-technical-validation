1 - The depth rule as submitted
-------------------------------
Submitted wording: two polygons means deep-seated, one polygon means
shallow, no exception. Landslides that contradict it, by type pair:

source+deposit  landslides
--------------  ----------
df+xdf          79        

landslides flagged by the submitted rule 79
Every one is a debris flow. No other type pair appears, which is why
the exception is specific to debris flows and not to flows in general,
and why the correction belongs in the text and not in the data.

None of these is a defect in the deposited data, and none is counted
in the integrity table of script 09, which measures the data. They are
corrections the checks forced on the manuscript itself, and they are
the strongest evidence that the validation was not circular: a check
that only restated the rules used to make the data could not have
contradicted them.
