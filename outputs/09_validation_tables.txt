The remainder is not a residue of unresolved errors. It is of three kinds.
'convention': the record follows a mapping rule that the Methods state
explicitly, namely that debris flows are classified shallow
whatever their polygon structure and that a landslide may need more than
two polygons. 'as mapped': the record was examined in the GIS and found
correct, for instance a long narrow debris-flow channel that a shape index
flags as a sliver, or a gap that is a transit zone between a detachment
area and its deposit. 'documented in the tables': a code that the
data contains and the published dictionary did not list.

different scope: the previous one mapped active, mostly gully-related
instabilities over 16.75 km2, the present one also maps old and very old
deep-seated landslides over the whole catchment. The informative figure is
the share of the previous inventory that the new one recovers.
